Present the IMML **north-star card** — a focused reminder of *why* this work exists, so the session stays
anchored. This is orientation only: **do not** start work, plan, or evaluate a task — just render the card.

The mission and the "why" are durable (below, fixed). For **Now** and **Open threads**, read the current
`CLAUDE.md` ("Current state (handoff)" + "Backlog / next work") and distil the few items genuinely in
flight — don't dump the whole backlog; pick the 3–6 that are active or next.

Render it compactly, roughly like this:

> 🎯 **Make Bittensor incentive mechanisms legible** — readable, comparable, checkable, simulatable, and
> *visually* clear — and always **surface, never hide**, the one bespoke part: the metric (the hole).
>
> - **Now** — ⟨current focus, 1–2 lines, from CLAUDE.md "Current state"⟩
> - **Open threads** — ⟨3–6 in-flight / next items from the backlog, e.g. graph the metric internals from
>   the vocab specs; the economics/demand facet (#5); the anatomy-diagram colour hint⟩
> - **Why** — legibility is the cure to the information-asymmetry / "market for lemons" problem: the market
>   can't allocate to subnets it can't read. The extended *whole-subnet* language (backlog #5) will make a
>   subnet's **value** legible too, not just its mechanism.

Keep it to a short card. Finish by offering to pick up one of the open threads — but don't begin until asked.
